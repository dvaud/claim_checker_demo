### New

- **Documents, grouped as you choose, with a filter.** The Documents page now groups its list: by who may read the
  documents with AI (the first time), by their role (evidence or theory), by what needs a look, or not at all. Each
  group has a head that stays in view while it scrolls, with a count, one sentence on what it means and *Select these
  N*, which becomes *Clear these N* once they are all ticked. A box above the list keeps the documents whose title or
  file name holds the words you type. A long title ends in "…" on its line and shows whole when the line is opened;
  a closed line shows its marks as small icons (a warning, something to check in its reading, theory, a wait for the
  owner), each named when you point at it and said in words in the panel the line opens. When a document's setting changes it moves to its new group without the page jumping, and glows where it
  lands; after changing several at once, their ticks are cleared and the message has *Undo* (which asks first when
  it would let Claude read documents again). *3 documents need a look* now has *Show them*.
- **Made for long lists.** Above the list, the groups' counts take you to each group ("Claude 266 · Local AI 27 · No
  AI 14"); a group's head stays under the page's top while its documents scroll, and folds away (remembered per
  project); the bar for the ticked documents stays at the bottom of the window, says how many of them the filter or a
  fold hides, and a line above the list leads to it. A line shows its file name when another document has the same
  title; the list sorts by title or newest first. Once a project has documents,
  *Add documents* is one line that opens.

Nothing here changes results.
