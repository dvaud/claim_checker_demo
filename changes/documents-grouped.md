### New

- **Documents, grouped as you choose, with a filter.** The Documents page now groups its list: by who may read the
  documents with AI (the first time), by their role (evidence or theory), by what needs a look, or not at all. Each
  group has a head that stays in view while it scrolls, with a count, one sentence on what it means and *Select these
  N*, which becomes *Clear these N* once they are all ticked. A box above the list keeps the documents whose title or
  file name holds the words you type. A long title ends in "…" on its line and shows whole when the line is opened;
  the warnings on a line are a word or two ("very short", "read badly", "check its reading"), said in full in its
  panel. When a document's setting changes it moves to its new group without the page jumping, and glows where it
  lands; after changing several at once, their ticks are cleared. *3 documents need a look* now has *Show them*.
  Nothing here changes results.
