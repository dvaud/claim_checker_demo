Rush hour is back in Rivermouth, despite home working

Rivermouth Daily (fictional), 12 March 2025

Traffic jams on the ring road are almost as long as before the pandemic, the regional traffic office reports. Although many office workers stay home on Mondays and Fridays, the roads are busier than ever from Tuesday to Thursday.

"Remote work has not reduced congestion on the days that matter," a spokesperson said. Freight traffic and online shopping deliveries have grown, filling the space left by commuters.

Environmental groups say that the climate benefit of home working is real but small, and that it does not replace investment in public transport.

Public transport operators report the same pattern. Trains into the city are half empty on Fridays but overcrowded on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, when many employers ask staff to come in. "We cannot run fewer trains on quiet days and more on busy days without more staff and rolling stock," said a spokesperson for the regional rail company.

The province is now talking to large employers about spreading office days across the week. A pilot with three employers starts in September.
