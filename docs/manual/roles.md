# Roles, proposals and what waits for your word

> **In short**
> - Open the *Team* tab: **Waiting for your word** is its first section, shown only when something waits. What needs a
>   decision of yours is there as a card, the most serious first. Roles and the hand-over are on the *Team page*.
> - A Member's change to the documents is a proposal. The owner and the Co-owners answer it. When two of them answer
>   differently at once, neither answer counts and one of them decides again.
> - Nothing loosens who may read a document with AI on your computer without your click.
> - A second computer of yours is a second person in your projects.

This page continues [Work together](together.md#the-owner-and-proposals-0170), which says what the three roles are.

## In the Team tab

The first section is **Waiting for your word**, with a number; it is shown only when something waits. It holds
everything that needs a decision of yours, as cards, the most serious first: a proposal answered both ways, a change of
owner you did not expect, a change made in your name that you did not make, more AI offered to you, then the ordinary
proposals. Under the cards is a quiet list, *Settled at the same time*. The owner's switch **Accept documents
collaborators add, at once** is on the Team page, on its *Owner* card. When nothing waits, the section is not there.

A problem with the shared folder that stops this copy (another computer writing under your name, the folder not
reachable) comes before even this section.

**Proposals.** Each card says who proposes what and when, how much it matters (*Low*, *Medium*, *High*, in words),
and what happens when it is accepted. When two proposals concern the same document (one colleague removes it, another
leaves a passage of it out), each card says so.

- **The owner and the Co-owners** answer with **Accept** or **Decline…**. *Decline…* asks for a *Reason (optional)*:
  the colleague sees it with the answer, and the research log keeps it. Accepting a removal or a looser AI setting
  asks first, with the same optional reason: *Remove "…" from every copy of the project?*
- **The Member who proposed it** sees the card under **Your proposals** as *Waiting for …* and can **Take back** the
  proposal: their copy stays as it is. When it was declined, it is shown first, with the reason, until they press
  *Mark as seen*.
- **Everyone else** sees the proposals, but cannot answer them.
- **Answered** (a fold) keeps the latest ten: *Accepted by …*, *Declined: …*, *Taken back*, *No longer needed: …*
  (the document was removed since, say). The research log keeps every one.
- The owner's switch **Accept documents collaborators add, at once** (the Team page, *Owner* card) stays the owner's.
  Off, each added document waits for a click. Removing a document and letting more AI read one always wait for an
  answer.

The *Team* tab's number counts what waits for your word, and a colleague's declined proposals you have not seen yet.

**A new reading that was accepted** is made in each copy by itself, as a job that waits for its turn, and every
result moves with it to the same words. When some result of yours would not find its place in the new reading, your
copy keeps the document as it is and the *Team* tab says *The project now reads "…" with the new reader*, with why:
open the document to look, or press **Move it anyway** (results that find no place are kept as records).

### A proposal answered both ways

Two of you, each with the right to answer, may answer one proposal differently before either sees the other's answer:
Anna accepts it and Ben declines it. ENLENS then picks neither. In the owner's and every Co-owner's copy a red notice
inside the proposal's card, first in the tab, says it: *Answered both ways. Anna accepted it and Ben declined it,
neither knowing the other's answer. Neither answer counts: the document stays in the project. Decide it again.* The
last sentence before *Decide it again* says what holds meanwhile, by kind:

| Proposal | While it waits |
| --- | --- |
| added a document | it is not one of the project's documents yet |
| removed a document | the document stays in the project |
| let more AI read | who may read it with AI stays as it was |
| read again | the project reads it as before |
| passages left out or restored | the passages read stay as they were |

- **What comes back in your copy.** What the accept had changed goes back. A document a removal took away comes back,
  from the copy kept for it. A looser AI setting goes back to the stricter one. A document that was added stays, as
  a waiting addition does, but is not yet one of the project's. A new reading that cannot go back stays in the copy
  that made it, and the document's mark says *Read with the newer reader here; the project decides again*. Your copy
  says so once, in a notice and in the research log.
- **Decide it again** with *Accept* or *Decline…* on the card, both small, neither the main button. *Accept* still
  asks first for a removal and a looser setting. Both people may use them. The later answer, given knowing both,
  counts.
- **A Member, and the person who proposed it,** see the card's state *Waiting: answered both ways, to be decided
  again*.
- **When one of the two becomes a Member again** before it is decided, that does not decide it either: the owner's
  own answer is set aside too, and the card says that person's answer no longer counts and the proposal must be
  decided again.

### Changes of owner you did not expect

Who the owner is never depends on whose file is newer. Your copy follows one owner for each place in the chain and
follows another only by your word. A **hand-over by the owner you follow** just happens, with a notice (*Anna handed
the project over to Ben: Ben is the owner now.*). Anything else comes to *Waiting for your word* as a card:

- **A take-over** (tag *A take-over*): *Ben took the project over: "Nothing was heard of the owner for 14 days." You
  follow Anna. If you follow Ben, Ben decides about the documents from now on.* **Follow Ben** or **Not now** (*Not
  now* leaves it in the list, and it no longer counts in the tab's number). In the owner's own copy it says *Here you are still the owner.*
- **Two records for one place.** A record your copy follows is never replaced by a file's. When a file holds another
  record of the same person for the same place (another owner after Anna, say), it is asked about like another first
  owner, and your copy goes on following the one it follows. Should the record your copy follows ever be gone from it,
  your copy follows no other one for that place until you say which.
- **Another first owner**: *This file names another first owner: Ben. You follow Anna.* **Follow Ben** or **Keep
  Anna**. When two people say they are the project's first owner and you follow neither yet: *Which do you follow?*
  with **Follow Anna** and **Follow Ben**.
- **A change in your name was refused.** A file that says you did something you did not do is not believed: nothing
  is changed in your copy, and the card says so. *Carol's file says you handed the project to Carol. You did not:
  nothing was changed here.* **Take the project back** (it writes a take-over of your own, which every copy is asked
  about) or **Dismiss**. For a document: *Carol's file held a change to "Jobs" in your name. It was not taken, and
  your own word was sent again.* **Dismiss**.

### More AI is offered, never applied

When the owner or a Co-owner lets more AI read a document, your copy does not follow by itself. For each person who
loosened something, one card says so (*Ben lets more AI read 2 documents*). Each document has a tick box that starts
unticked, and shows both settings (*Here: Only a local AI · Ben's: Claude may read it*). The buttons wait until you
tick, and then count: **Allow here for 2 documents** or **Keep 2 stricter here**. With one document there is no box:
**Allow here** or **Keep it stricter here**. A document you leave alone stays stricter here. A stricter setting still
holds everywhere at once, with no question.

Two cases need no card, because the click was yours: a looser setting you made yourself as the owner or a Co-owner
(your copy holds it as its own), and the very setting your own accepted proposal asked for. A later loosening, also of
a document you once asked to open, is offered like any other.

### Removed in the last 30 days

A document the owner or a Co-owner removes, or one whose addition was declined, leaves your copy at once, and is kept
aside for 30 days. Under **Removed in the last 30 days** each row gives the title, who removed it and why (*By Anna
(owner) on 12 November: "reason". Can be restored until 12 December.*) and **Restore**. A person who restores sees
*"Jobs" is back in the project.*; a Member sees *"Jobs" is back in your copy, and proposed to the owner.* After 30
days the copy kept aside is deleted and the document cannot be restored. Your own removal of a document in your own
copy is not kept this way.

### Settled at the same time

Two people who may change the documents can change one thing without each other's version. Nothing is lost by chance,
and nobody is asked first: ENLENS keeps what is safe and lists it for the owner and the Co-owners under **Settled at
the same time**, one quiet row each, with **Leave it so** (it writes the setting again as it is, so the row goes in
every copy). The document's title opens it, to change it.

| Both of you changed | What holds |
| --- | --- |
| who may read it with AI | the stricter setting |
| removed against kept | the document stays. *To remove it, use its menu on Documents.* |
| read again with the new reader | the newer reader's reading |
| which passages are read | the choices of the person with the larger id hold, with yours on top where they differ |

## The people list: roles and the hand-over

This is on the *Team page* (*Manage the team* in the project menu). *People* names each person's role in the person
mark: **Owner** or **Co-owner**; a Member has none. Under the list's head one sentence tells you yours: *You are a
Co-owner: your changes to the documents hold for everyone, and you answer proposals.* or *You are a Member: your changes
to the documents are proposals to Anna and the Co-owners.*

In the owner's copy each other person's row has a **More** menu, with three items (four when your copy keeps a storage
space's keys: *Remove from the team…*, last), each of which asks first:

- **Make a Co-owner…** (or **Make a Member again…**): *Ben's changes to the documents will hold for everyone at once,
  and Ben can accept or decline proposals. Only you set roles and hand the project over.*
- **Same person as…**: *Is Anna de Vries (laptop) the same person as someone here?* One person on two computers
  stands together in the list, and a judgement of theirs counts once. Rows of one person stand together; the second
  line of the second computer's row says *Also Anna de Vries: the same person* (or *Also you: …* in your own copy).
- **Make owner…**, last, after a line: *Hand this project over to …? They will decide about its documents from now on;
  proposals that wait become theirs to answer. You stay in the team.*

A Co-owner whose ENLENS is older than this release has the tag *needs a newer ENLENS to act as Co-owner*. In a team that
works with files only (no shared folder), the *Owner* card names the owner (*Owner: …*), and the owner's *Hand over…* is
there.

**Taking over.** When the owner has left (their subfolder is gone from the shared folder, or nothing was heard of them
for 14 days), a Co-owner's *Team* tab says why and offers **Take over** (and the *Owner* card offers **Become the
owner** when the project has none). A Member is offered it only when no Co-owner has been heard of for 14 days either.
While the owner is there, only they can hand the project over. A take-over is never final for the others: each other
copy is asked, as above, and follows only by its person's word.

## On the Documents page

- A document a colleague added that waits for an answer is marked **Waiting for the owner**; your own changes that
  wait too. A document of yours the owner declined is marked **In your copy only**: it stays with you, and no other
  copy brings it back.
- In a Member's copy, *Remove* says *In your copy it goes now; for the project it is a proposal to the owner.*

## The question when you import a move file

When the file is one that [moves ENLENS to another computer](together.md#moving-to-another-computer), ENLENS imports
nothing until you answer.
**This file moves ENLENS from another computer**, it says, with what is in it (*3 projects, made on 12 November with
ENLENS 0.17.1, by Dávid.*) and two cards:

- **I am moving to this computer.** The other computer stops. You stay the same person here. Offered only when this
  computer has no projects yet; otherwise a line under the cards says *This computer already has projects, so it keeps
  its own name and settings.*
- **I will use both computers.** This computer becomes a second person in your projects. The owner can mark both as
  you. Give *Your name on this computer* (it suggests your name with (2)).

Under the cards the switch **Take the settings too** has a fold that lists them. A server address that is not on
this computer, for what ENLENS reads or for text recognition, has its own tick box, off: *Send what ENLENS reads to
https://… as the other computer did.* Nothing is taken that you did not tick: an address of another computer's
server is never taken silently, and a back end left without its address goes back to its default. **Bring in** waits
until you choose a card. Each project keeps its name (`name (2)` when the name is taken).

## A second computer is a second person

If you use two computers, each is a person in your projects. Copying your ENLENS folder to another computer does not
make one: the copy would write into the shared folder under your name, and the two would write over each other. So
ENLENS stops it before anything is written. The Team tab says *Another computer is writing into this folder under
your name (since 12 November). Nothing was written from here, so nothing is lost.* Press **Use both computers…**,
give a name, and this computer becomes a second person. What you did on it so far stays here, shown as your other
computer's.

In the owner's copy the new person then appears under *People* on the Team page (which also offers *Use both
computers…*). Use *More → Same person as…* there to mark your two computers as one person: they stand together, and a
judgement of yours counts once.

## When a Co-owner becomes a Member again

What the project took from them stays. What they change from then on, and what they had changed but not sent yet,
comes as proposals. A proposal they had answered differently from someone else waits to be decided again. A known
limit: a stricter AI setting they made just before may stay in a copy it had already reached, stricter than the
project, until that copy's person changes it (it errs on the strict side).

## Linking a folder made by someone else

*Open an existing ENLENS project folder* on the *Projects* page (or `use_folder_as_project` in Claude Code) uses a
folder ENLENS made earlier in place. When the folder was made by someone else, ENLENS asks first: **This folder is
Anna's project.** *ENLENS will clean it like a project brought in from a file: its record of a shared folder, file paths
on the other computer, and what was stored for speed go. The documents and the work stay.* **Clean and link** or
**Cancel**.
